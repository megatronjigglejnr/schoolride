SchoolRide - notes for later
============================

THE BUS IS THE BRAND MARK
-------------------------
Decided (Nov 2026): the flat bus from the Track screen is SchoolRide's mark. Use the same drawing
everywhere, so the app reads as one thing from the home screen to the live ride.

Where it is used now
- Start-up animation: the bus sits on the marigold field (the native splash shows the same frame), the
  "SchoolRide" wordmark rises in, the engine starts (a shiver and exhaust puffs), the bus rocks back nose-up,
  then speeds off to the right with a big puff and speed lines, and the field fades into the first screen (~2.1s).
  Prototype: .intro in app/styles.css and startUp() in app/app.js.
  App: mobile/src/components/Intro.tsx.
- Native splash image: mobile/assets/images/splash-bus.png (rendered from the #bus sprite at 3x,
  528 x 240, transparent), on marigold #F5B82E, 176pt wide (mobile/app.json, expo-splash-screen).
- Track: the bus rides the white sheet's top edge, and a small one moves along the mini-map.
- Onboarding welcome screen: the bus above the wordmark.

- The current icons in mobile/assets/images (icon.png, the android-icon-* files and expo.icon) are
  still the Expo template's; replace all of them.
- iOS: 1024 x 1024, no transparency, the bus centred with generous margin (the system rounds the corners).
