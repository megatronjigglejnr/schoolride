      Last updated 3 November 2026

      Draft for the prototype. The final text comes from the school network's legal team before release.

      1. Who provides SchoolRide

      Your child's school licenses SchoolRide and runs its transport. We provide the app that shows you that transport.

      2. Who can use it

      Guardians the school has verified. The school links each guardian to their children; you can't link a child yourself.

      3. What you see

      Only your own children's rides: the bus's position, ETAs, pickups and drop-offs, and exam-day changes. Other children on a route appear only as a number of stops.

      4. Sharing

      You can share a ride's live ETA with your co-guardian or a trusted family friend. A share lasts for that day only. Collecting a child still needs the school's approval.

      5. Your data

      We keep your name, phone number, email, linked children, ride history and settings to run the service. We don't use your phone's location. You can request a copy of your data at any time under Account, Privacy.

      6. Times are estimates

      ETAs depend on traffic and the bus's signal. Always follow the school's instructions on the day.

      7. Changes

      If these terms change, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
